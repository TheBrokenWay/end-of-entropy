# THE END OF ENTROPY: Deterministic Quantum Stability via Energy-Primary Invariance

## What it is
Quantum computing is limited by **decoherence**: quantum states decay through contact with their surroundings. Standard quantum mechanics calls this the unavoidable work of entropy (the steady growth of disorder), because it treats time as a fixed background.

## About this work
This paper records the author's theory at the time it was written. It is published as part of his body of work, to show how his ideas developed over time. It is not presented as a proven or peer-reviewed result.
